Our story

The beginning

MIZU began with a bowl in a Kyoto tea house and a simple question: why does a morning drink feel so rushed? We set out to bring that unhurried bowl home.

From Japan

We buy directly from growers in Uji and Kagoshima, whose families have farmed tea for generations. Each harvest is tasted by us before it ships.

Slow is a better flavour.

The leaf

Tea plants are shaded for three weeks, hand-picked, steamed, dried and de-stemmed into tencha, then ground slowly between granite stones.

Quality and questions

What makes matcha ceremonial grade?

Young, shaded first-flush leaves, stone-ground to a fine, sweet powder.

How should I store it?

Sealed, in the fridge, away from light. Use within 8 weeks of opening.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to over 30 countries. Free shipping over $60.
